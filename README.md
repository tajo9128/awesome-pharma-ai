# Awesome Pharma AI 🧬🤖

> A curated list of **free** resources for AI in pharmaceutical research, drug discovery, and healthcare — courses, tools, datasets, and communities.

**Contributions welcome!** PR your favorite free resource.

---

## 🎓 Free Video Courses

### AI & Machine Learning
- [AI & ML Foundations](https://learn.biodockify.com) — core ML concepts, neural networks, Keras, NLP (BioDockify Learn)
- [Deep Learning with PyTorch: Zero to Production](https://learn.biodockify.com) — tensors → CNNs → Vision Transformers → deployment (adapted from Daniel Bourke's MIT-licensed course)
- [Deep Generative Models: GANs, VAEs & Diffusion](https://learn.biodockify.com) — hands-on generative modeling notebooks
- [AI in Drug Discovery](https://learn.biodockify.com) — deep learning for target identification and lead optimization

### LLMs & Agents
- [The Complete LLM Engineering Bootcamp](https://learn.biodockify.com) — architecture, fine-tuning, RAG, agents, deployment (adapted from mlabonne's Apache-2.0 course)
- [AI Agents Crash Course](https://learn.biodockify.com) — tool calling, memory, RAG, multi-agent systems

### Pharmacy & Drug Discovery
- [Introduction to Pharmacology](https://learn.biodockify.com) — drug mechanisms, PK/PD
- [Pharmacokinetics & Drug Metabolism](https://learn.biodockify.com) — ADME, CYP450, dosing
- [Molecular Docking with AutoDock Vina](https://learn.biodockify.com) — hands-on docking simulations
- [Structural Biology & Drug Discovery](https://learn.biodockify.com) — proteins to structure-based design
- [Medicinal Chemistry Essentials](https://learn.biodockify.com) — SAR and lead optimization

### Bioinformatics
- [Bioinformatics & Computational Genomics](https://learn.biodockify.com) — sequencing, alignment, variant calling
- [Computational Biomedicine: Command Line to Single-Cell](https://learn.biodockify.com) — CLI, R, genomes, scRNA-seq with Seurat

### Healthcare AI
- [AI in Healthcare: Diagnosis to Drug Discovery](https://learn.biodockify.com) — the full landscape, explainable AI (SHAP, LIME, GradCAM), governance, and when to override an AI system
- [Python for Pharmaceutical Research](https://learn.biodockify.com) — pandas & RDKit for drug data

## 🛠️ Tools & Software
- [AutoDock Vina](https://vina.scripps.edu/) — molecular docking
- [RDKit](https://www.rdkit.org/) — cheminformatics toolkit
- [Seurat](https://satijalab.org/seurat/) — single-cell RNA-seq analysis
- [DeepChem](https://deepchem.io/) — deep learning for chemistry
- [PyTorch](https://pytorch.org/) — deep learning framework

## 📊 Datasets
- [PDB](https://www.rcsb.org/) — protein structures
- [ChEMBL](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/) — bioactive molecules
- [TCGA](https://www.cancer.gov/tcga) — cancer genomics
- [Cellxgene](https://cellxgene.cziscience.com/) — single-cell data

## 👥 Communities
- [r/bioinformatics](https://www.reddit.com/r/bioinformatics/)
- [BioStars](https://www.biostars.org/) — bioinformatics Q&A
- [r/DrugDiscovery](https://www.reddit.com/r/DrugDiscovery/)
